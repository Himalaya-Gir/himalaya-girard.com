From piloting to behavioural testing. This is where theory meets human decision-making.

The experimental pipeline

STAGE 1PILOTING
Long enough to estimate, short enough to finish
Two pilots fixed the length of the session and the wording of the instructions. The final design: 400 trials, the same 200 in each condition, about 90 minutes in total with the other tasks.

What the pilots tested

  1. –Which trials are most informative in practice
  2. –How to keep participants engaged throughout
  3. –What instructions actually make sense to real people
  4. –Comprehension and attention checks that work
STAGE 2BEHAVIOURAL EXPERIMENT
The preregistered study
100 analysable participants planned, each playing both conditions for real stakes. The next phase: the sample, the session and the planned analyses.
