PHASE 05DATA COLLECTION

The
Test

The preregistered behavioural study, at the Donders Institute. Data collection in progress.

The question

Can Prospect Theory explain trust decisions? Or does the social context change the parameters?

Recruitment

Target100 participants, aged 18 to 35
WhereDonders Institute, Radboud University
Sessionabout 90 minutes, paid, with a bonus
THE SESSION

Three tasks, then questionnaires

Investment task

5 trials. Allocate 0 to 10 tokens to a future participant.

Reciprocation task

36 trials as trustee. Receive an investment, then choose between two amounts to return.

Inverted Trust Game

400 trials, 200 social and 200 non‑social, in alternating blocks of 50. Invest, or not.

Then: Social Value Orientation, General Trust Scale, Moral Foundations Questionnaire-2, and a short self-report

Planned analyses

PRIMARY
Model comparison
Twenty candidate models fitted per participant by maximum likelihood, compared by BIC. Then, for each parameter the winning model lets differ, a paired test of the social minus non-social difference.

The hypotheses H1 to H4 are those of phase 2.

OPEN SCIENCE
Hypotheses, design, exclusions and analyses are preregistered on OSF, before anyone looked at the data
