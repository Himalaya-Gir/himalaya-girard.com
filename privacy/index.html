Privacy

This site and That One Paper are run by Himalaya Girard. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Visiting this site

Visit statistics are counted with Umami Cloud. It uses no cookies and does not identify you. It records the page you open, the site you came from, your country, and your type of device, only as totals.

Videos

Videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; the player then comes from youtube-nocookie.com, and Google's privacy policy applies.

The That One Paper publishing tool

That One Paper uses a small tool, run on Himalaya's own computer, to publish its own videos and posts to its own YouTube channel and social accounts. When it is connected to Google, it only uses YouTube access to upload and manage videos on the That One Paper channel and to read that channel's statistics. It does not access anyone else's data. Nothing it receives is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is stored only on that computer.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.

Writing to me

If you email me, I use your message only to reply.
