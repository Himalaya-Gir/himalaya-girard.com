I'm Himalaya Girard, PhD student in Psychology at Yale
We act differently depending on who we are with, and we spend much of our lives imagining what other people believe, want, and would do. I am interested in the models we build of other minds and in how flexible they are: when someone becomes interchangeable because of the role they play, how we fill in what we do not know about a person, and how far we can step outside our own mind.
